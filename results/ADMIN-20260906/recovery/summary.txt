The victim is SIGKILLed ten times with a two second outage between
each, while the witness consumer on another port is never touched.

Connect-to-first-frame is timed from the connection being established,
not from process start: exec and dynamic linking belong to whatever
restarts the consumer, and charging them to the gateway would measure
the wrong thing.

The witness line is the one that matters. A gateway that stalls, drops
or resequences for its other consumers when one of them dies has not
isolated them, whatever the recovery time says.
